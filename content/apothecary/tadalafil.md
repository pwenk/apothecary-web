The most famous sex drug in the world does nothing to desire. Put a man on it with no one and nothing he wants, and nothing happens.

What it does is stranger and smaller. It keeps one chemical message from being erased. By the end of this page you'll know which message, how a failed heart trial in Wales led to it, and the one medicine that turns the same pill dangerous.

## A gas that relaxes muscle

An erection is a plumbing event run by a gas. When you're aroused, nerves in the penis and the lining of its blood vessels release nitric oxide, NO. The gas slips into the smooth muscle around the arteries and spongy chambers and switches on an enzyme that makes a messenger called cGMP.

cGMP tells the muscle to let go. The arteries widen, the chambers fill like a sponge in a bath, and the swelling presses shut the veins that would drain them. Blood comes in faster than it can leave.

Robert Furchgott, Louis Ignarro and Ferid Murad shared the 1998 Nobel Prize for showing that a simple gas carries signals in the blood vessels. **The body runs one of its most intimate events on something close to car exhaust.**

Every signal needs an off switch. This one has a very efficient one.

## The enzyme that wipes the message

PDE5, short for phosphodiesterase type 5, breaks cGMP down. It's the eraser that ends an erection once the nerves go quiet. Tadalafil sits in PDE5's working pocket and blocks it, so cGMP lingers longer and builds higher while you're aroused.

That's the whole trick. No arousal means no nitric oxide, no cGMP and nothing for the drug to protect. **Tadalafil turns up the volume on a song that has to be playing already.** It doesn't change libido, attraction or the wish for sex, and trials measure erections, not desire. When the problem is wanting, the answer is elsewhere: in hormones, mood, medicines, the relationship, or in the drug from the last essay.

The idea of blocking PDE5 for sex came out of a laboratory that wasn't looking for it.

## The angina accident

In the early 1990s Pfizer chemists in Sandwich, Kent, were testing a PDE5 blocker called UK-92480 for angina, the chest pain of starved heart muscle. As a heart drug it disappointed. Volunteers in early studies in Swansea mentioned another effect, and in 1996 a small British trial confirmed that the drug improved erections in men with erectile dysfunction.

It became sildenafil, Viagra, approved in the US in March 1998. Lilly and the biotech company ICOS brought tadalafil, Cialis, in 2003.

> Viagra was a heart drug that failed upward.

The two drugs do the same job. What sets tadalafil apart is time.

## Why they called it the weekend pill

Sildenafil's half-life, the time for blood levels to fall by half, is around four hours. Tadalafil's is about 17.5 hours in healthy men. In the trials behind its label, a single tablet still improved erections compared with placebo up to 36 hours later. Friday night to Sunday morning, roughly, hence the nickname.

That long tail allowed a second way to use it: a small daily dose that keeps a steady level, so timing stops mattering. It also opened the door to other jobs.

Tadalafil relaxes smooth muscle in the prostate and bladder neck too. In 2011 the FDA approved it for the urinary symptoms of an enlarged prostate, the weak stream and night-time trips of older men; pooled trials found real if modest improvements. Under another brand it treats pulmonary arterial hypertension, high pressure in the lungs' arteries, where relaxing vessel walls is the point.

Its PDE5 grip isn't perfectly exclusive. Tadalafil also brushes against PDE11, found in muscle, and back pain and muscle aches a day after a dose are its signature side effect. Sildenafil leans more on PDE6 in the retina, which is why some men briefly see the world tinted blue.

## What the Berbarian Wizard noticed

Jamal Dinkoui, the Berbarian Wizard, collects tadalafil findings beyond the bedroom. He cites a 2006 Italian pilot in which 20 men with erectile dysfunction used tadalafil for a year: estradiol fell by about a sixth and the testosterone-to-estradiol ratio rose, with no placebo group. He also cites a cycling study in which tadalafil blunted the brief rise in DHT after all-out exercise.

He reads both carefully. The pilot had no control group and inconsistent units, and he writes that it would not make tadalafil his choice for high estrogen. The DHT result changed a blood level for an hour and proved nothing about the enzyme behind it. Evidence for the hormone effects: thin.

His most useful warning is about supplements. In one analysis he cites, 74 of 91 products sold as natural sexual enhancers contained sildenafil, tadalafil or an unapproved look-alike. Many "herbal" boosters work because they're secretly this drug, at an unknown dose.

## The nitrate danger

Nitroglycerin and other nitrate medicines for angina work by releasing nitric oxide. Combine them with tadalafil and the same signal floods every blood vessel at once: cGMP piles up, vessels go slack everywhere, and blood pressure can drop far enough to faint, have a heart attack or die. That's why nitrates are an absolute contraindication.

Because tadalafil lingers, the label says that in a life-threatening emergency at least 48 hours should pass after the last tablet before a nitrate is even considered. Paramedics and emergency doctors need to know. So do men who use "poppers," amyl nitrite inhaled for sex, which works on the same pathway.

## Before you try anything

Erection trouble can be the first sign of blocked arteries, diabetes or low testosterone, so it deserves a check-up before a prescription. Tell your doctor about nitrates, poppers, blood-pressure and prostate drugs called alpha blockers, and strong antifungal or HIV medicines that slow tadalafil's breakdown. An erection lasting more than four hours, sudden loss of vision or sudden loss of hearing needs emergency care.

Tadalafil works on the plumbing and leaves desire alone. The next molecule is rumoured to touch desire, hair, thyroid and mood all at once, sold in little bottles as a skin oil. It shares nearly all its letters with a boar's sex scent and almost none of its effects.
